How My Crypto Faucet Works

My Crypto Faucet is a Bitcoin faucet that allows registered users to claim small BTC rewards at regular intervals. This page explains the basic process from registration to withdrawal.

1. Create an Account

Start by creating your account with a valid email address and password. Your account gives you access to the faucet dashboard, where you can claim rewards and manage your balance.

2. Claim Your Bitcoin Reward

Step 1: Sign in to your account and open the faucet dashboard.

Step 2: Complete the required security verification.

Step 3: Submit your claim when the faucet is available.

Step 4: Your Bitcoin reward is added to your faucet balance after a successful claim.

The faucet uses a cooldown between claims. Once a claim has been completed, you need to wait until the next eligible claim period.

3. Check Your Balance

Your dashboard shows your available Bitcoin balance. You can also review your previous claims and withdrawal activity from your account.

4. Request a Withdrawal

When your balance reaches the minimum withdrawal amount, you can request a Bitcoin withdrawal from the dashboard.

You must provide a valid Bitcoin address associated with FaucetPay for the BTC withdrawal process.

Always check your Bitcoin address carefully before submitting a withdrawal request. Cryptocurrency transactions cannot normally be reversed after they are sent.

5. Withdrawal Processing

Withdrawal requests are checked before processing. A valid request is placed into the withdrawal process and its status can be followed from your account.

Processing time can depend on the payment service and network conditions.

6. Referral Rewards

My Crypto Faucet also provides a referral system for users who want to invite genuine new members to the platform. Each user can find their personal referral link from the referral section of the dashboard.

Referral rewards are based on milestones reached by the referred user. When an eligible milestone is completed, the corresponding referral reward is added automatically to the referrer's Bitcoin balance.

Referral rewards are designed for genuine user referrals. Attempts to create or operate accounts for abusive or fraudulent referral activity may result in account restrictions.

Claim and Withdrawal Details

The current faucet reward is 0.00000001 BTC for each successful claim, with a one-hour claim interval.

The current minimum Bitcoin withdrawal amount is 0.0000001 BTC. Eligible withdrawals are processed through FaucetPay and require an accepted FaucetPay BTC address.

Reward amounts, claim conditions and service features may be changed in the future. Always check the information displayed on your account before making a claim or withdrawal.

Security and Fair Use

My Crypto Faucet uses security checks designed to help protect accounts and prevent automated or abusive activity.

Users should keep their login credentials private and use only their own account. Attempts to abuse the service or bypass security controls may result in account restrictions.
