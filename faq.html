Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about My Crypto Faucet, Bitcoin rewards, claims, balances, withdrawals and account security.

What is My Crypto Faucet?
My Crypto Faucet is a Bitcoin faucet platform where registered users can claim small BTC rewards at the available claim interval and build their account balance.
How often can I claim Bitcoin?
Eligible users can make a faucet claim once every 1 hour. After a successful claim, the cooldown period must finish before another claim becomes available.
How much Bitcoin do I receive from a claim?
The current faucet reward is 0.00000001 BTC per successful claim. Faucet conditions and reward settings may change in the future.
Do I need an account to use the faucet?
Yes. You need to create an account and sign in before you can use the faucet claim system and manage your balance.
What is the minimum withdrawal amount?
The current minimum withdrawal amount is 0.0000001 BTC. Your available balance must meet the minimum before a withdrawal request can be submitted.
How do Bitcoin withdrawals work?
When your balance reaches the minimum withdrawal amount, you can submit a BTC withdrawal request from your dashboard. The Bitcoin address is checked before the withdrawal request is created.
Why do I need a FaucetPay Bitcoin address?
My Crypto Faucet uses FaucetPay for eligible Bitcoin withdrawals. Therefore, the withdrawal address must be a valid FaucetPay BTC address accepted by the payment system.
Can I use any Bitcoin address for withdrawal?
No. A Bitcoin address can be technically valid but still not be registered as a FaucetPay BTC address. The withdrawal system checks the address with the payment service before creating the request.
Why can't I claim my reward right now?
A claim may be unavailable when your cooldown period has not finished or when a required security verification has not been completed successfully.
Is My Crypto Faucet available worldwide?
The platform is designed for users worldwide. Availability of individual services or payment providers can depend on their respective terms and regional restrictions.
How can I protect my account?
Use a strong, unique password and never share your login details with another person. Keep access to your email account secure and contact support if you notice unusual activity.
How can I contact support?
Logged-in users can use the Contact Support feature available from their account dashboard to send a support message.
How do referral rewards work?
You can invite genuine new users using your personal referral link. Referral rewards are based on milestones reached by the referred user, and eligible rewards are added automatically to the referrer's BTC balance.
Is there a limit to the number of referrals I can make?
There is no fixed limit on the number of genuine users you can refer. However, referral activity must follow the platform's security and fair-use requirements.
When are referral rewards added?
When a referred user reaches an eligible referral milestone, the corresponding reward is processed automatically and added to the referrer's BTC balance.
Important: Cryptocurrency transactions involve risk. Always check your withdrawal details carefully before submitting a request.
