Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information My Crypto Faucet may process when you create an account, use the faucet, request a withdrawal, contact support, or use security features on the website.

1. Information We Process

Depending on how you use the service, information may include:

  • your email address and account information;
  • authentication and session information needed to keep your account signed in securely;
  • security and device-related information used for account protection and abuse prevention;
  • Bitcoin claim and withdrawal information, including the amount, currency, status, and wallet address you submit for a withdrawal;
  • support messages and information you provide through the support feature;
  • password-reset and verification information needed to protect account recovery; and
  • security verification information from services such as Cloudflare Turnstile.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to create and manage accounts, authenticate users, process claims and withdrawals, verify security requirements, prevent abuse and fraud, provide support, maintain the website, troubleshoot technical problems, and protect the service and its users.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

My Crypto Faucet uses necessary cookies or similar browser storage mechanisms for functions such as maintaining a secure login session and supporting security controls. These technologies are used to operate the service rather than to provide a general advertising profile.

4. Bitcoin Withdrawals and FaucetPay

When you request a BTC withdrawal, the wallet address, amount, currency, and information necessary to process the withdrawal may be sent to FaucetPay. FaucetPay may process that information according to its own services, terms, and privacy practices.

5. Cloudflare Turnstile

The website uses Cloudflare Turnstile as a security and anti-abuse measure. Turnstile may process technical information needed to determine whether a request appears to come from a legitimate user. Its processing is governed by Cloudflare's own policies.

6. Account Security

Authentication information is used to protect your account. Passwords should not be shared with anyone. If you believe your account has been compromised, use the available account recovery or support features as soon as possible.

7. Data Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers when this is necessary to operate or secure the website. For example, withdrawal information is sent to FaucetPay for BTC processing and security verification may involve Cloudflare Turnstile. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where necessary to protect the service, users, or rights of others.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for account operation, security, transaction records, support, dispute handling, legal obligations, and legitimate operational needs. Retention periods can vary depending on the type of information and the reason it is kept.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live and which privacy laws apply, you may have rights concerning your personal information, such as rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or data portability. Requests may be subject to legal exceptions and identity verification.

10. Children

The service is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children who are not legally permitted to use the service in their jurisdiction. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact support so the situation can be reviewed.

11. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect account and service information. However, no internet service or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, services, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

13. Privacy Requests and Support

If you have a privacy question or need help with your account information, please use the support feature available after signing in. Please provide enough information for your request to be understood, but do not send passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.

This page describes the website's current privacy practices in plain language. It is not legal advice and may need to be updated as the service, providers, or applicable privacy requirements change.
